### About

I am an undergraduate mechanical engineering student at Carnegie Mellon University (Class of 2030) pursuing a minor in Robotics. My work focuses on end-to-end hardware development—from CAD architecture and fabrication to field validation.

**Currently at CMU:**
- **Airlab** — Designing and fabricating ruggedized, modular sensor payloads for Rubicon Robotics ATVs.
- **Carnegie Mellon Moon Miners** — Modeling drivetrain and chassis CAD assemblies and running drawbar-pull testing for Lunabotics rovers.
- **TartanAUV** — Redesigning a waterproofed underwater tether spool and prototyping perfboard sensor validation circuits.
