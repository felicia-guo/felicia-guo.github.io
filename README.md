## About

I’m a first-year mechanical engineering student at Carnegie Mellon (Class of 2030). Recent work includes leading the mechanical team on an **XPRIZE Wildfire finalist** drone project, helping launch **two experiments to the International Space Station**, and building VEX competition robots since 2021.

Right now I’m designing sensor payloads for ATVs at **Airlab**, modeling rover drivetrains and chassis with **Carnegie Mellon Moon Miners**, and building electronics for an underwater vehicle with **TartanAUV**.
